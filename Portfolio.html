CASE-001 · PRD & growth strategy shipped

Growing smallcase MTU.

CASE-001 · 2023 smallcase · Monthly Transacting Users @ Upraised CAP
PROBLEM
How do you grow Monthly Transacting Users at a curated investments platform by 15%, without leaning entirely on paid acquisition?
APPROACH
Mapped the activation and retention funnel. Looked at competitor behaviour across adjacent fintech apps. Prioritised levers by impact vs. effort. Wrote a full PRD with hypotheses, success metrics and a phased rollout plan.
OUTCOME
A prioritised growth strategy with explicit success metrics and a sequence that engineering could plan against. Shipped as part of the Upraised CAP.
STACK
PRDFunnel analysisCompetitor teardownSuccess metrics
